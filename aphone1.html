Aphone — Concept 01

A hypothetical flagship phone, staged as a single hero product shot with studio lighting and a full CGI campaign frame.

Turntable + hero shot walkthrough of the Aphone Concept 01 render.

About this project

This project marked my first major experiment with Blender, as I had previously only created still image renders rather than animated videos. By following online tutorials and instructional videos, I learned the fundamentals of animation in Blender and successfully animated a 3D smartphone model. After rendering each scene, I imported the footage into Adobe Premiere Pro, where I edited and assembled the clips to produce the final advertisement.
